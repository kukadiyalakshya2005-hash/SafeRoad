Wear Helmet

A helmet protects your head and reduces the risk of serious injury during accidents. Wearing a helmet while driving can save lives and improve road safety for everyone.

🚗

Seat Belt

A seat belt keeps the driver and passengers secure during sudden stops or accidents. Wearing a seat belt reduces the risk of serious injury and increases road safety.

📵

Avoid Mobile

Using a mobile phone while driving reduces concentration and increases the risk of accidents. Keep your eyes on the road and stay focused to ensure safe driving for yourself and others.

🚦

Follow Signals

Traffic signals help control vehicle movement and maintain order on the road. Following traffic signals reduces accidents and ensures safety for drivers and pedestrians.
