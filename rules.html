🚦 Traffic Rules

Follow these rules for a safer journey.

🪖 Wear Helmet

Always wear a helmet while riding to reduce serious head injuries.

🚗 Wear Seat Belt

Seat belts protect passengers during sudden stops and accidents.

📵 Avoid Mobile

Do not use mobile phones while driving to stay focused.

🚦 Follow Signals

Traffic signals maintain order and reduce accidents.

⚡ Control Speed

Drive within speed limits to prevent collisions.

🍺 No Drunk Driving

Never drive under alcohol influence.

🚶 Respect Pedestrians

Stop at crossings and allow pedestrians safely.

↔ Maintain Distance

Keep safe distance between vehicles.

🌧 Drive Carefully

Reduce speed during rain and poor visibility.

🔧 Vehicle Maintenance

Regularly check brakes, tyres and lights.

🚫 No Wrong Side Driving

Wrong side driving increases the risk of serious accidents.

🔄 Use Indicators

Use turn indicators before changing lanes or turning.
